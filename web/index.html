How It Works

From raw customer data to a profitable retention decision.

1. Predict who will churn

An XGBoost model (tuned with 5-fold cross-validation, compared against a logistic-regression baseline) estimates each customer's churn probability from contract, tenure, services, billing and payment data.

2. Explain why

SHAP values break every prediction into per-feature contributions, so each customer gets their own top risk factors, not just a global feature ranking.

3. Decide who to target, with uplift modelling

High risk does not mean a discount will help. An S-learner estimates how much the offer changes each customer's churn probability. Customers are grouped into:

    An offer is sent only when uplift × monthly bill × 12 − discount cost exceeds a $20 margin.

    4. Honest caveat

    Public churn data has no record of past offers, so the offer experiment is semi-synthetic: real customers and churn baselines, with a simulated randomized offer whose true effect is known. That lets the targeting be scored exactly. In production you would train the uplift model on a real A/B pilot. Individual uplift estimates are noisy; the model is most reliable for ranking groups of customers.
